# About The Lore

## Discover knowledge your team already has

The Lore is a shared library of reusable AI skills designed to help people find proven ways to complete their work.

Teams develop valuable prompts, processes, and working methods every day—but that knowledge is often scattered across conversations, documents, and individual notebooks. The Lore gives that knowledge a home where it can be discovered, reused, improved, and shared.

Instead of beginning every task with a blank prompt, you can describe what you want to accomplish and find a skill created for that type of work.

---

## What is a skill?

A skill is a reusable AI workflow that helps you complete a specific task like summarizing meetings, drafting documents, analyzing feedback, or creating project plans. 

It may capture:

- A reliable prompt or workflow
- Instructions for approaching a task
- Questions the AI should ask before starting
- Important team knowledge or lessons
- Common mistakes to avoid
- The expected structure of the result
- Examples showing how the skill should be used

Skills are more than saved prompts. They package practical knowledge into a format that other people can understand and reuse.

For example, a skill might help you turn meeting notes into action items, prepare a project update, review a user experience, design a website logo, or improve the quality of a software project.

---

## Skills available in The Lore

The Lore contains a growing collection of skills across several areas.

### Meetings and planning

Meeting skills help turn conversations into useful outcomes rather than leaving important information buried in notes or transcripts.

They can help you:

- Summarize unstructured meeting notes
- Identify decisions and key takeaways
- Extract action items and owners
- Convert meeting transcripts into weekly or monthly plans
- Organize dependencies, milestones, and unresolved questions
- Prepare follow-up information after a discussion

These skills are useful for project kickoffs, planning sessions, customer calls, retrospectives, standups, and team meetings.

### Team communication

Communication skills turn rough notes into messages that are clear, concise, and ready to share.

They can help you:

- Draft a team update
- Write a weekly status report
- Summarize completed work
- Communicate blockers and risks
- Explain next steps
- Prepare an update for a manager, mentor, or project group

The goal is to help people communicate useful information without spending unnecessary time rewriting it.

### Presentations and storytelling

Presentation skills help organize ideas into a clear story for an audience.

They can help you:

- Create a presentation outline
- Choose a logical order for the content
- Explain a problem, approach, and outcome
- Highlight impact and lessons learned
- Prepare presentation talking points
- Adapt a message for a particular audience

These skills are especially useful when you know what you want to say but need help turning it into a focused narrative.

### Internship and professional growth

The Lore includes skills that help interns and early-career contributors keep track of their work and communicate their impact.

They can help you:

- Build a record of accomplishments
- Turn project activity into progress updates
- Identify lessons and evidence of growth
- Create stronger résumé bullet points
- Prepare review or impact narratives
- Remember work completed across several weeks or months
- Develop presentation material from real project contributions

These skills help transform everyday tasks into a clearer account of progress, learning, and impact.

### Design and user experience

The design collection supports many stages of creating and improving digital experiences.

Skills can help with:

- Establishing a product’s audience, personality, and design direction
- Planning a new interface
- Improving layout, spacing, and visual hierarchy
- Making a design feel clearer or more confident
- Improving labels, instructions, messages, and other interface text
- Reviewing onboarding and first-time experiences
- Adapting an experience for different screen sizes
- Adding thoughtful animation and interaction
- Making an interface feel more memorable and enjoyable
- Checking accessibility and usability
- Reviewing consistency and overall quality
- Completing a final polish before launch

The catalog also includes a website logo design assistant. It can help explore logo directions, define visual themes, consider typography and color, and prepare a practical brief for producing the final assets.

### Technical work

Technical skills support people who build and maintain software projects.

They can help you:

- Review code quality
- Identify patterns and possible issues
- Find opportunities for improvement
- Suggest areas for simplification or refactoring
- Understand complicated sections of a project
- Review consistency with project standards
- Organize files and project resources

These skills are intended to provide structured, practical assistance rather than generic suggestions.

## Finding the right skill

You do not need to know the exact name of a skill before using The Lore.

Simply describe what you are trying to accomplish. For example:

- “Turn these meeting notes into action items.”
- “Help me write this week’s project update.”
- “Create a plan from this customer call.”
- “Review the usability of this page.”
- “Give me some logo directions for my website.”
- “Help me explain the impact of my internship project.”

The Lore compares your request with the skills in its library and recommends the ones that best fit your goal.

You can also browse the complete catalog, explore categories, open a skill to learn more, and save useful skills to your favorites.

---

## Using a skill

Each skill includes a description of what it does and guidance for completing the task.

After finding a useful skill, you can:

1. Open it to understand its purpose.
2. Review when and how it should be used.
3. Copy or download it for your own work.
4. Adapt it to your project or team.
5. Save it as a favorite so it is easy to find again.

Some skills provide a short, focused process. Others offer a more detailed workflow for tasks that require planning, review, or multiple decisions.

You remain in control of the final result. Skills provide a strong starting point, but you should review and adapt their outputs for your situation.

## Creating and sharing skills

The Lore is not only a place to find skills. It is also a place to contribute them.

If you have developed a useful workflow, prompt, or method, The Lore can help you turn it into a reusable skill. A guided conversation helps capture details such as:

- The problem the skill solves
- Who it is intended for
- When it should and should not be used
- The information it requires
- The result it should produce
- Important lessons or common mistakes
- A real example of the workflow
- Ways other teams could adapt it

You can review and refine the draft before sharing it with others.

This makes it easier to preserve knowledge that might otherwise remain with one person or disappear when a project ends.

## How The Lore works

The Lore is built as an interactive website connected to an intelligent skill library.

The experience has three main parts:

### A simple website experience

The website provides a central place to discover, browse, create, save, and reuse skills. Personal features help you return to favorite skills, recently viewed items, and skills you have contributed.

### Intelligent discovery

The Lore considers the meaning of your request rather than relying only on exact keywords. This allows it to recommend relevant skills even when your wording is different from the wording used by the skill’s author.

For more complex requests, the discovery guide can help clarify what you need and recommend the most suitable skill.

### A shared skill catalog

Every skill is stored in a consistent, portable format. This makes the catalog easier to search, share, update, and expand as teams contribute new knowledge.

Together, these parts create an experience that feels like searching a library of practical team expertise.

---

## Why The Lore exists

Useful knowledge is often difficult to find.

A team member may have already created an excellent prompt, checklist, or workflow, but other people may not know that it exists. As a result, teams repeatedly solve the same problems, produce inconsistent results, and lose valuable lessons over time.

The Lore is designed to reduce that repetition.

It helps teams:

- Reuse approaches that already work
- Make valuable knowledge easier to discover
- Share expertise across projects and roles
- Help new team members become productive faster
- Create more consistent results
- Preserve lessons learned from real work
- Improve shared workflows over time

The goal is not to replace human expertise. It is to make that expertise easier to capture and available to more people.

## A growing community library

The Lore becomes more valuable as people use and contribute to it.

Every shared skill can give another person a better starting point. Every improvement can make a workflow clearer and more useful. Over time, the catalog can grow into a living library of the knowledge, practices, and lessons that help teams do their best work.
