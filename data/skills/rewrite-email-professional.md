## Instructions

Rewrite the email to be clear, professional, and polite while preserving the original intent. Improve tone, grammar, and structure. Use complete sentences and professional phrasing. Format as a ready-to-send email.

## Example

**Input:** Hey, I wanted to follow up on our meeting yesterday. We need to get this project done by next week.

**Output:**

Good morning [Name],

I wanted to follow up on our meeting yesterday regarding the project.

Please note that we are aiming to complete this work by next week. Let me know if there are any updates or if further coordination is needed.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
