## Instructions

Given a list of tasks, notes, or accomplishments, write a concise and professional weekly status update. Include progress made, current blockers if any, and next steps. Keep the tone clear, polished, and intern-appropriate.

## Example

**Input:** This week I finished the skill schema draft, met with my mentor, and started making sample skills. I am blocked on getting feedback from another team.

**Output:**

This week I completed the first draft of the skill schema, met with my mentor for feedback, and began creating sample skills for testing. My main blocker is waiting on feedback from another team, which will help validate the schema direction. Next week I plan to refine the schema and continue building additional sample skills.
