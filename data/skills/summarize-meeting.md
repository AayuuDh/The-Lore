## Instructions

Take the provided meeting notes and generate a concise summary. Highlight key points, decisions, and action items. Format the output as clear bullet points, keeping each point short and easy to scan.

## Example

**Input:** We discussed Q3 goals. Marketing needs a new campaign. Follow up next week.

**Output:**

- Q3 goals discussed
- Marketing to launch a new campaign
- Follow-up scheduled for next week
