## Instructions

Given a presentation topic, audience, and goal, generate a clear outline with a logical flow. Include a suggested title, key sections, and short descriptions of what each part should cover. Keep the structure concise and presentation-ready.

## Example

**Input:** I need to present my internship project and explain the problem, the solution, and what I learned.

**Output:**

Title: Improving Skill Discovery and Reuse

- Problem: Why finding and reusing skills is difficult today
- Approach: How the project explored a structured solution
- Progress: Key work completed so far
- Impact: Why this matters for teams and users
- Learnings and Next Steps: What was learned and where the project could go next
