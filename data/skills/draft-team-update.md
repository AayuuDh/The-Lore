## Instructions

Given notes about completed work, current progress, blockers, and next steps, write a concise and professional team update. Keep the tone clear and collaborative. Format the output as short bullet points or a brief paragraph that is easy to paste into Teams, Slack, or email.

## Example

**Input:** I finished reviewing sample skills, started cleaning up the schema, and found a problem with inconsistent tags. Next I want to standardize the format and get feedback from my team.

**Output:**

- Completed review of sample skills and began refining the schema structure
- Identified an issue with inconsistent tagging across entries
- Next steps: standardize tag formatting and share updates with the team for feedback
