## Instructions

Identify and extract clear action items from the input text. Write each action as a concise task, optionally including who is responsible and any deadlines if mentioned.

## Example

**Input:** We need John to finalize the budget by Friday. Marketing should prepare campaign draft next week.

**Output:**

- John to finalize budget by Friday
- Marketing team to prepare campaign draft next week
