## Instructions

Write a clear and constructive feedback message. Keep the tone professional and supportive. Highlight positives, provide specific suggestions, and avoid overly harsh language.

## Example

**Input:** The presentation was too long and confusing.

**Output:**

The presentation covered important points, but it could be improved by simplifying some sections and shortening the overall length to make it more engaging.
