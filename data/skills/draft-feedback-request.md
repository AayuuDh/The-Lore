## Instructions

Given the situation and the type of feedback needed, write a polite and professional feedback request. Make the request specific enough to guide a useful response, and keep the message concise and respectful of the other person's time.

## Example

**Input:** I want to ask my manager for feedback on whether my project updates are clear and whether I am focusing on the right priorities.

**Output:**

Hi, I wanted to ask if you would be open to sharing feedback on how I have been communicating project updates so far. I would especially appreciate any thoughts on whether my updates are clear and whether I am focusing on the right priorities. Thank you.
