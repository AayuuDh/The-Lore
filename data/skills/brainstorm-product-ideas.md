## Instructions

Given a problem or domain, generate a list of creative and diverse product ideas. Focus on practicality, uniqueness, and user value. Format the output as a concise bullet list, with each idea clearly described in one line.

## Example

**Input:** We need a new app for managing personal finances.

**Output:**

- AI-powered budgeting assistant that categorizes spending automatically
- Goal-based savings app with visual progress tracking
- Subscription tracker that alerts users before billing cycles
- Financial wellness app with personalized insights and tips
