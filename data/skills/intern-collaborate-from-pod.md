## Problem

The intern pod — a room of desks — makes collaboration physically awkward.
Interrupting someone feels rude, so interns stay blocked in silence and drift
into isolation. That silence reads as "not engaged," which is the opposite of the
truth.

## Audience

Microsoft interns who want to communicate and collaborate more but find the
physical/social setup makes reaching teammates hard.

## Team / Org Context

Grounded in the intern desk-pod reality and Microsoft's **Teams**-first, async
communication norms — how you actually reach a busy senior engineer without
hovering at their desk.

## Workspace Context

Teams (chats, channels), your team's async update habits, and your list of who
owns what.

## Embedded Knowledge

- "Ask early, ask specifically" — a crisp, easy-to-answer question gets fast help
  and makes you look sharp, not lost.
- Being visibly communicative is itself return-offer signal; silence isn't
  humility, it's a missed impression.
- **Why a generic AI can't do this:** it's tuned to the intern pod dynamic and the
  fear of interrupting, and it drafts the real message — not generic "communicate
  clearly" advice.

## Required Inputs

- What you're blocked on or want to share.
- Who you'd reach (role is enough) and how formal your team is.

## Workflow / Instructions

1. Tell it what you're stuck on or want to broadcast.
2. It reshapes it into a specific, respectful, easy-to-answer message.
3. It nudges you to actually send it (and to timebox before asking — but not
   suffer for hours).
4. It helps you post a short async update so the team sees your progress.

## Expected Outputs

- A ready-to-send Teams message, a clearer question, and an async update that
  keeps your team looped in.

## Common Pitfalls

- Staying blocked for hours out of not wanting to bother anyone.
- Over-apologizing or burying the ask; it trims that.
- Vague "can someone help?" messages; it forces specifics.

## Example Usage

You've been stuck for two hours and don't want to interrupt. You say "help me ask
without sounding dumb." It turns your ramble into: "Hey `[name]` — stuck on X,
here's what I tried and the error; is it Y or Z? 2 min when free?" You send it and
get unblocked.

## Adaptation Notes

Works for any remote/awkward-to-collaborate setup, not just intern pods.
