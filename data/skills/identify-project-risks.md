## Instructions

Given a project description, identify the main risks, blockers, or dependencies that could affect success. Focus on practical concerns such as unclear scope, missing data, technical uncertainty, stakeholder alignment, or timeline pressure. Format the output as a concise bullet list.

## Example

**Input:** We need to create a demo-ready skill library in a short timeframe with evolving requirements and multiple collaborators.

**Output:**

- Scope may grow too quickly beyond what is realistic for the timeline
- Requirements may continue to change, causing rework
- Inconsistent formatting across collaborators could reduce quality
- Limited validation with real users may weaken confidence in the final demo
